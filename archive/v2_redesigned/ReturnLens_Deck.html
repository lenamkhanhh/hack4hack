A Wrong Decision Costs Both Ways
• Unjust Approval: Loses money, shipping costs, and inventory to wardrobing or swapped items.
• Unfair Denial: Destroys customer goodwill, loses repeat buyers, and invites harsh marketplace disputes.
Sourced Industry Benchmark
Fraud estimates differ by definition: 9% (NRF / Happy Returns 2025, fraud only) vs 15.14% (Appriss / Deloitte 2024, fraudulent & abusive claims rate).
*U.S. retail estimates; not measured on our target sellers.

The gap is not a missing returns portal. It is that no single record ties order data, video frame, policy clause, and decision together.

Architecture & Safety · Deterministic Control

Policy checks and seller approval control actions

01 · Observe

Models Inspect Evidence

Models process buyer messages and server-extracted video frames.

  • Outputs structured visual observations
  • Identifies damage timestamps and tags
  • Constraint: Models observe, but observations can be imperfect and are never final verdicts
02 · Check

Deterministic Policy & Critic

Pure code evaluates server-enforced rules; LLMs cannot override.

  • Code Policy Engine: Delivery date, 7-day window, exact SKU match, refund cap calculation
  • Independent Critic: Reviews cited frames and flags unsupported observations
  • Fail-closed rule: Discrepancy triggers escalation
03 · Authorize

Mandatory Seller Gate

Human-in-the-loop sign-off required for all irreversible steps.

  • No automated refunds or label generation
  • Presents 10-second proof package
  • Draft created in pending_return_receipt
  • Immutable JSONL audit trail of all steps
DECISION FUNCTION: Contradiction → Escalate | Hard Fail → Escalate | Unclear Evidence → Ask for Photo | All Pass → Seller Review
